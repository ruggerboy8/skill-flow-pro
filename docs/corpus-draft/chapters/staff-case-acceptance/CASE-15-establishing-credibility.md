# CASE-15 Establishing Credibility

Role: Front Desk (DFI) | Domain: Case Acceptance | Framework code: DFI.CASE 3

## Overview

This competency belongs to the Front Desk (DFI) team. [ALCAN] It is about building patient trust by effectively communicating the practice's and dentists' expertise, experience, and commitment to quality care, and highlighting qualifications, advanced technologies, or accolades to reassure families of the high standard of treatment they will receive.

[ALCAN] The framework's tagline: "Make sure they know we are the best."

## Why it matters

[ALCAN] "You are the team's biggest fan. When you highlight our doctors' expertise, you give patients the peace of mind that they are in the best hands."

[GENERAL] Trust in the doctor is built before the family ever meets the doctor. A specific, genuine detail from you at booking ("she trained at a children's hospital," "he's great with anxious kids") does more for case acceptance than anything printed on the wall, because it comes from a person who knows the team.

## The moves

### Move 15: Preview the doctor by name

[ALCAN] "I always preview the patient's doctor by name, and comment specifically on that doctor's expertise or experience when scheduling an appointment."

[ALCAN] Drop the doctor's name and one genuine detail about their skill. It makes families feel secure and reminds them they're in good hands. The coaching note: building the doctor up builds the parent's confidence. Pick one specific strength for each doctor (for example, "great with anxiety") and use it in your next scheduling call.

### Move 52: Know our technologies and why they matter

[ALCAN] "I am able to list the innovative technologies, materials, and processes our practice uses and explain how they lead to positive health outcomes."

[ALCAN] Families love hearing what makes your office stand out. Share it with pride; your confidence helps them trust the quality of care. The coaching note: you don't need to be an engineer, just a fan. Ask a Lead, "What is one cool thing about our laser/scanner that I can tell parents?" and use that fact the same day.

### Move 93: Preview OVERJET on new patient calls

[ALCAN] "I always preview our use of OVERJET as the most 'advanced AI powered xray technology' when explaining the flow of a new patient visit."

[ALCAN] When you talk about OVERJET, keep it simple and confident. It tells families your team uses the best tools for their child's care, no tech talk required. The coaching note: "AI" sounds scary, "precision" sounds safe. Ask the doctor to show you an OVERJET image so you can see exactly what you are describing to parents.

## What good looks like / common pitfalls

[GENERAL] Good looks like: every booking call includes the doctor's name plus one true, specific credibility detail, delivered like you are bragging about a friend, not reading a brochure.

[GENERAL] Common pitfalls:
- Generic praise ("all our doctors are great") instead of one specific detail.
- Over-teching the OVERJET pitch; the point is precision and early detection, not how AI works.
- Inventing or exaggerating credentials. If you don't know a doctor's background, find out before you preview it.
