# CASE-16 Facilitating Smooth Processes

Role: Front Desk (DFI) | Domain: Case Acceptance | Framework code: DFI.CASE 4

## Overview

This competency belongs to the Front Desk (DFI) team. [ALCAN] It is about quickly and confidently providing patients and guardians with all necessary resources, documentation, and materials in a timely manner, and making the process of scheduling and undergoing treatment as smooth and efficient as possible, which enhances patient satisfaction and promotes follow-through with recommended care.

[ALCAN] The framework's tagline: "Creating Magic - make it a magical experience."

## Why it matters

[ALCAN] "You make the logistics invisible. By handling the paperwork and details effortlessly, you let the patient focus entirely on their health and happiness."

[GENERAL] Friction kills follow-through. Every form completed before arrival, every expectation set at booking, is one less reason for a family to run late, feel flustered, or postpone treatment. This competency is the quiet engine behind case acceptance: families say yes more easily when everything around the yes feels easy.

## The moves

### Move 16: Point guardians to the patient portal

[ALCAN] "I always inform the guardian about completing paperwork via the patient portal when scheduling or confirming appointments."

[ALCAN] Give them a head start. A quick reminder about the portal saves time at check-in and makes the visit feel effortless for everyone. The coaching note: front-loading paperwork saves everyone's sanity. Ask a Lead to show you the Portal Status view so you can spot missing forms before the patient walks in.

### Move 53: Preview the excellent visit

[ALCAN] "I always verbally preview the excellent service to expect during an exceptional visit when booking an appointment."

[ALCAN] Give them a glimpse of what's ahead. A confident preview sets high expectations and shows that great service is standard here. The coaching note: set the stage. Saying "You're going to love Dr. Smith" creates a placebo effect of a good visit. Say it to the next patient you book.

### Move 94: Confirm portal login before ending new-patient calls

[ALCAN] "For all new-patient booking calls, I direct the caller to their patient portal, and confirm they have been able to successfully log in before ending the call."

[ALCAN] Before you hang up, make sure they can log in. One minute now saves five later and keeps paperwork from piling up at the front desk.

**Portal reminder (move 16):**
> "You should be receiving an email with a link to your patient portal here in a few minutes. Please take some time to go through these forms before your kiddo's appointment to ensure there aren't any delays in their appointment time!"

**Visit preview (move 53):**
> "Awesome, so we've got you fully booked. You can expect us to do a full exam and cleaning, take x rays so that we can have a baseline for your kiddo's oral health, and you'll be spending time with Dr. Mary who will help outline what our continued care might look like. We're really excited to have you joining us."

**Portal login confirmation (move 94):**
> "Before we finish, go ahead and check your email for a link to your patient portal. I'll stay on the line to make sure you can get in. Got it? Awesome, you'll want to complete all tasks here prior to arrival, that will help us make sure your visit runs on time."

## What good looks like / common pitfalls

[GENERAL] Good looks like: no new patient ends a booking call without a working portal login and a clear picture of their first visit; no family hears about a form for the first time at check-in.

[GENERAL] Common pitfalls:
- Mentioning the portal but not confirming the family can actually get in.
- Treating the visit preview as optional filler and skipping it when busy.
- Waiting until day-of to chase missing paperwork instead of spotting it ahead of time.
