### Move 184: Benefits language, never "covered" or "out-of-pocket"

[ALCAN] "I always use the language 'estimated insurance benefits' and 'estimated family contribution,' and I never use the words 'covered' or 'out-of-pocket' when discussing costs with parents."

## What good looks like / common pitfalls

[GENERAL] Good looks like: the same money vocabulary shows up in every conversation, from booking call to checkout, because you use it, coach it, and correct drift gently in the moment.

[GENERAL] Common pitfalls: reverting to "covered/not covered" when rushed; letting different team members quote costs with different vocabulary; quoting estimates as promises.
