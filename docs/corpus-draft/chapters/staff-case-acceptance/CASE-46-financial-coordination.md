## Overview

This competency belongs to Office Managers. [ALCAN] The framework's tagline is "Make money talks easy," and its friendly description: "You make sure families get accurate estimates, verified benefits, and clear payment options, so finances support rather than block care."

## Why it matters

[ALCAN] Offering clear, flexible payment options helps parents say yes to needed treatment without feeling pressured or stuck.

[GENERAL] When cost conversations reach the office manager, they are usually the hard ones. Your calm command of every option is often the difference between a child getting treatment now and a plan quietly dying in "we'll think about it."

## The moves

### Move 185: Respond to financial concerns with all the options

[ALCAN] "I always respond to financial concerns with a clear explanation of all options including reasonably breaking up treatment and using CareCredit."

[ALCAN] The framework's practice note: when a parent hesitates over cost, slow down and walk through each payment option calmly and clearly.

## What good looks like / common pitfalls

[GENERAL] Good looks like: every financial concern gets the full menu, staging treatment reasonably and financing, explained without judgment, and the family leaves with a concrete plan rather than a vague promise to follow up.

[GENERAL] Common pitfalls: offering only one option; letting staging decisions drift into clinical territory without the doctor's prioritization; treating a cost concern as a lost case instead of a coordination problem.
