### Move 188: Model one over-the-top check-in every day

[ALCAN] "I always personally complete the full check-in process with at least one family each day, modeling over-the-top hospitality by greeting them by name, confirming comfort, offering amenities, and setting clear expectations for the visit so the team sees the standard we expect."

[ALCAN] The framework's practice note: choose one family each day to check in yourself and use that moment to model the warmth and clarity you expect from the team.

## What good looks like / common pitfalls

[GENERAL] Good looks like: the team sees you do the full check-in, unhurried, at least once a day, and can name the four ingredients (name, comfort, amenities, expectations) because they watch you deliver them.

[GENERAL] Common pitfalls: only modeling when the lobby is calm; doing a partial check-in and calling it modeling; treating it as a performance for the team rather than genuine hospitality for the family; skipping the debrief so the team never learns what they were meant to notice.
