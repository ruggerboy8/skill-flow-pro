## Why it matters

[GENERAL] The Office Manager is the only person positioned to see the whole practice in numbers: production, collections, reviews, new patients. A weekly rhythm of reviewing those numbers and committing to specific actions is what separates managing a practice from merely reacting to it.

## The moves

### 168. Weekly KPI review with an action plan to your Regional Manager

[ALCAN] "I always review last week's key KPIs every Monday (i.e. production, collections, Google Reviews, and New Patient #'s), identify problem areas, and send my Regional Manager an action plan to address."

[ALCAN] Framework guidance: "A weekly KPI review with an action plan keeps the practice focused on the numbers that matter and turns issues into concrete steps, not just awareness." And the practical tip: "Protect time every Monday to review KPIs and send a short, specific action plan email to your Regional Manager."

[GENERAL] The move has three parts and all three count: review the numbers, name the problem areas honestly, and send a plan with specific actions. A review that ends at "noted" is only a third of the move.

## What good looks like / common pitfalls

[GENERAL] **What good looks like:**
- The Monday review happens on protected time, every week, even after a rough weekend.
- The email names problems plainly and pairs each with a concrete action, an owner, and a date.

[GENERAL] **Common pitfalls:**
- Sending numbers without a plan, or a plan so vague ("do better on reviews") it cannot be checked next Monday.
- Skipping the review in busy weeks, exactly when the numbers most need attention.
