# CLER-38: Schedule and Flow

**Role:** Office Manager | **Domain:** Clerical | **Competency:** 38 | **Tagline:** [ALCAN] "Smooth arrivals to goodbyes"

## Overview

This competency covers the Office Manager's accountability for the daily communication and scheduling engine. [ALCAN] Its friendly description: "You actively manage the schedule, staffing, and room flow so patients move through smoothly, minimizing waits, confusion, and bottlenecks."

It applies to the Office Manager role. A distinctive feature of these moves: most say "by either me or a team member" or "by either myself or my team." The Office Manager does not have to do every task personally, but owns the outcome either way.

## Why it matters

[GENERAL] Many of these standards mirror front desk moves (the 3-ring phone standard, 48-hour confirmations, paperwork chasing; compare CLER-6 and CLER-7). The front desk versions describe doing the work; these describe making sure it happened. When both layers run, no call, message, or online request goes unanswered, and no family arrives unconfirmed or unprepared.

## The moves

### 169. Clear the VoiceStack "Potential Opportunities" column daily

[ALCAN] "I always take action on every item in the VoiceStack 'Potential Opportunities' column before the end of the day."

[ALCAN] Framework guidance: "Acting on VoiceStack opportunities daily prevents missed treatment, callbacks, and follow-ups that would otherwise leak revenue and hurt patient experience." Tip: "Schedule a short block near the end of each day to clear or assign every 'Potential Opportunities' item."

### 170. Own the three-ring phone standard

[ALCAN] "I always ensure the phone is answered by either me or a team member within 3 rings."

[ALCAN] Framework guidance: "Holding the team to a three-ring standard reduces missed calls and gives parents confidence that the office is responsive and organized." Tip: "Review call reports regularly and coach team members when the three-ring standard isn't being met."

### 171. Ensure documented confirmations for the next 48 hours

[ALCAN] "I always ensure that all appointments scheduled within the next 48 hours have a documented confirmation (call, text, or email) by either myself or my team."

[ALCAN] Framework guidance: "Confirming upcoming appointments 48 hours in advance reduces no-shows and last-minute gaps that hurt production and flow." Tip: "Build a daily 48-hour confirmation routine and check the report until it's consistently at 100%."

### 172. Catch missing paperwork 48 hours out

[ALCAN] "I always review the schedule 48 hours in advance and ensure families missing paperwork are contacted with clear instructions by either myself or my team."

[ALCAN] Framework guidance: "Catching missing paperwork ahead of time prevents bottlenecks at check-in and helps the day start smoothly for both staff and families." Tip: "Add a 48-hour paperwork review to your daily schedule and verify that every family has been contacted."

### 173. Close out Iris chats every day

[ALCAN] "I always review Iris chats at the end of each day and ensure all messages are responded to or assigned to a specific owner by either myself or my team."

[ALCAN] Framework guidance: "Closing out Iris chats daily keeps digital communication tight and prevents parent messages from slipping through the cracks." Tip: "Before leaving, open Iris and make sure every chat is either answered or clearly assigned."

### 174. Respond to every online appointment request daily

[ALCAN] "I always check for and ensure a response to all online appointment requests every day."

[ALCAN] Framework guidance: "Responding to online appointment requests daily shows parents you are responsive and prevents missed opportunities to fill the schedule." Tip: "Set a daily reminder to check online requests and make sure each one is scheduled, declined with a note, or set for follow-up."

## What good looks like / common pitfalls

[GENERAL] **What good looks like:**
- End of day means empty queues: no unactioned opportunities, no unowned chats, no unanswered online requests.
- Everything inside 48 hours is confirmed and paperwork-complete, and there is a documented trail proving it.
- The three-ring standard is coached with data from call reports, not enforced by vibes.

[GENERAL] **Common pitfalls:**
- Confusing "delegated" with "done": the moves allow the team to do the work, but the Office Manager still verifies the outcome.
- Answering some Iris chats but leaving the rest unassigned, so overnight they belong to no one.
- Checking the 48-hour report only on calm days, so the busy days that most need it slip.
