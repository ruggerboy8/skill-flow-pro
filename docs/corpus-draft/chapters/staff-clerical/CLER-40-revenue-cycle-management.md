# CLER-40: Revenue Cycle Management

**Role:** Office Manager | **Domain:** Clerical | **Competency:** 40 | **Tagline:** [ALCAN] "Keep cash flow healthy"

## Overview

This competency covers the Office Manager's ownership of the money pipeline. [ALCAN] Its friendly description: "You own the money pipeline, making sure claims, collections, and follow-ups happen consistently so the practice stays financially strong."

It applies to the Office Manager role.

## Why it matters

[GENERAL] Care that is delivered but never collected still costs the practice everything it took to deliver. The four moves here cover the cycle at its pressure points: balances handled before the visit instead of at the desk, daily production numbers that reconcile, and claim flags worked before they become a backlog. Getting ahead of balances is also a kindness to families: nobody enjoys a surprise conversation at check-in with their child watching.

## The moves

### 176. Review uncollected balances two business days before the appointment

[ALCAN] "I always review all uncollected balances two business days before the scheduled appointment."

[ALCAN] Framework guidance: "Reviewing uncollected balances ahead of visits allows you to plan for conversations and avoid surprises at the front desk." Tip: "Use a 2-day-ahead report to flag uncollected balances and note how each account will be handled at the visit."

### 177. Contact families about balances before they arrive

[ALCAN] "I always ensure families with uncollected balances are contacted by phone or text prior to their appointment to review payment expectations or arrangements."

[ALCAN] Framework guidance: "Proactively contacting families about balances reduces awkward day-of conversations and improves the chance you'll collect what is owed." Tip: "Make it a habit to reach out early about balances so families know what to expect before they arrive."

[GENERAL] Moves 176 and 177 work as a pair: the two-day review finds the balances, the outreach resolves them. The front desk has a matching move on the confirmation call (CULT-10, move 55: informing patients of outstanding balances during appointment confirmation).

### 178. Complete and reconcile the Daily Production Report before 10am

[ALCAN] "I always complete and submit the Daily Production Report and verify it matches production figures in Carestack before 10am the following day."

[ALCAN] Framework guidance: "Completing and reconciling the Daily Production Report keeps your numbers accurate and builds trust in the practice's reporting." Tip: "Block time each morning to finish the DPR and double-check it against Carestack before 10am."

### 179. Keep unactioned claim flags below 10

[ALCAN] "I always review claim flags daily and work or assign them so that unactioned flags remain below 10."

[ALCAN] Framework guidance: "Keeping claim flags low ensures claims move quickly, reducing delays in payment and preventing growing backlogs." Tip: "Check claim flags every day and don't let the unactioned count creep above 10."

[GENERAL] This daily working standard pairs with the weekly pattern analysis in CLER-39: work the flags daily, study why they keep appearing weekly.

## What good looks like / common pitfalls

[GENERAL] **What good looks like:**
- Every balance walking in within two business days has been reviewed and has a plan, and the family has already heard from the office.
- The DPR is finished, reconciled against Carestack, and submitted before 10am, every morning.
- The claim flag queue is a working list touched daily, with the unactioned count reliably in single digits.

[GENERAL] **Common pitfalls:**
- Letting check-in be the first place a family hears about a balance.
- Submitting a DPR that was never actually compared line by line against Carestack.
- Letting flags accumulate "until there's time," which is how a backlog becomes a project.
- Reviewing balances without noting how each account will be handled, so the front desk still improvises day-of.
