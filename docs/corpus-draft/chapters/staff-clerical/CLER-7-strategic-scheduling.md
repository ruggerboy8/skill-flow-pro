# CLER-7: Strategic Scheduling

**Role:** Front Desk (DFI) | **Domain:** Clerical | **Competency:** 7 (DFI.CLER 3) | **Tagline:** [ALCAN] "Full schedule, no cancellations"

## Overview

This competency covers building and defending the schedule. [ALCAN] The framework defines it as utilizing "all available resources to construct and maintain an optimal schedule that maximizes productivity and allocates dentist time effectively," ensuring "appointments are scheduled to meet clinic goals and patient needs, contributing to the practice's overall efficiency and success."

It applies to everyone in the Front Desk (DFI) role.

## Why it matters

[ALCAN] From the framework: "You don't just fill slots; you orchestrate the day. A strategically built schedule maximizes our impact and ensures the team works efficiently."

[GENERAL] An empty chair costs the practice the same as a full one, and a no-show wastes doctor time that another child needed. The moves here are the front desk's levers for a healthy schedule: confirm ahead, fill gaps aggressively, and run the weekly reports that catch problems before they become day-of surprises.

## The moves

### 6. Confirm the schedule two days ahead: call first, then text

[ALCAN] "I always examine the schedule two days ahead and first call, then send a personalized text message until all unconfirmed appointments are confirmed."

[ALCAN] Framework guidance: "Two days ahead is the sweet spot. A quick call, then a personalized text, keeps the schedule full and should help you feel some peace of mind." And: "This is the sweet spot. If you are calling day-of, you are already behind. Pull the 2 Days Out list today and make 5 calls."

### 75. Contact 10 patients per empty treatment slot

[ALCAN] "I always use the CareStack list function to identify and contact 10 patients per empty treatment slot."

[ALCAN] Framework guidance: "Use your tools like a pro. Ten quick contacts can turn an empty slot into a full day and make your schedule much healthier." And: "Volume is the cure for empty chairs. Don't just call one; call ten. Use the Quick Fill list in CareStack to burn through numbers faster."

### 103. Zero pending follow-ups in VoiceStack Opportunities by close

[ALCAN] "I always make sure there are zero 'pending follow up' items inside the VoiceStack Opportunities tab by the end of the day."

[ALCAN] Framework guidance: "End every day clean. When follow-ups are handled, the system, and the team, runs smoother." If it is piling up: "A clean dashboard is a clean mind. If Pending Follow-ups are piling up, ask the Office Manager to help you triage the list for 15 minutes today."

### 114. Pull and send the Same Day Verification report daily

[ALCAN] "Every day I pull the Same Day Verification report for the next 3 days and send it to Medusind."

[ALCAN] Framework guidance: "Staying ahead on insurance verification keeps appointments smooth and reduces day-of delays. A quick daily report ensures families are ready and the clinical team isn't stuck waiting." And, bluntly: "This prevents billing fires. If Medusind doesn't get this, we work for free. Make this the very first thing you do after logging in, even before checking email."

### 152. Pull the Interpreter Report every Monday

[ALCAN] "Every Monday I use CareStack to pull the 'Interpreter Report' for the next 2 weeks and schedule interpreters as necessary."

[ALCAN] Framework guidance: "Planning interpreters ahead of time removes stress for families and the clinical team. A quick Monday check ensures every patient feels supported and nothing gets missed at the last minute." And: "Monday automation sets up the week. If you forget this, we scramble later. If necessary, set a recurring reminder on your phone for Interpreter Audit at 9:00 AM on Mondays right now."

### 153. Pull the missing codes report every Monday

[ALCAN] "Every Monday I pull the missing codes report for the next 3 months and fill in all missing codes."

[ALCAN] Framework guidance: "Filling in missing codes early keeps schedules accurate and prevents last-minute surprises. A weekly sweep ensures records are complete and billing runs smoothly." Feeling overwhelmed? "Missing codes mean missing revenue. If scanning 3 months feels overwhelming, break it down. Do Next Month today, and the rest tomorrow. Just get started."

## What good looks like / common pitfalls

[GENERAL] **What good looks like:**
- Two days out, the schedule is fully confirmed; confirmations never start day-of.
- Every empty treatment slot triggers ten contacts, not one hopeful call.
- The daily Same Day Verification report goes out first thing, before email.
- Monday means two reports without fail: interpreters for the next 2 weeks, missing codes for the next 3 months.
- The VoiceStack Opportunities tab shows zero pending follow-ups at close.

[GENERAL] **Common pitfalls:**
- Texting only, and skipping the call-first step of the confirmation cadence.
- Stopping outreach after one or two contacts because "nobody answers."
- Treating the Medusind report as an end-of-day task, when the framework says it comes first.
- Letting the Opportunities tab carry a standing backlog that nobody owns.
