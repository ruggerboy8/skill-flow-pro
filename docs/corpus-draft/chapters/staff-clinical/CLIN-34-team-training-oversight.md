## What good looks like / common pitfalls

[GENERAL] What good looks like:

- Attendance is checked the same day as each meeting, not reconstructed at week's end.
- A missed meeting triggers a conversation about the barrier, not just a reminder to attend.
- Repeat misses by the same person become a coaching topic rather than background noise.

[GENERAL] Common pitfalls:

- Tracking attendance without following up, which teaches the team that missing has no cost.
- Framing the follow-up as policing rather than as protecting the person's development time.
