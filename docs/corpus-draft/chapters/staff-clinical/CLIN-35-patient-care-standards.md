[ALCAN] "I always schedule time each week to observe the RDA to Doctor hand-off, document at least one opportunity for improvement, and follow up with the appropriate team member about it."

## What good looks like / common pitfalls

[GENERAL] What good looks like:

- The observation is on the calendar, not left to "when things are slow."
- Feedback names one specific, small thing, and it reaches the person who can change it.
- Over weeks, the documented observations form a visible record of whether hand-offs are improving.

[GENERAL] Common pitfalls:

- Observing without documenting, or documenting without following up.
- Saving up feedback for reviews instead of delivering it the same week.
- Only ever watching the strongest RDA's hand-offs because their rooms run on time.
