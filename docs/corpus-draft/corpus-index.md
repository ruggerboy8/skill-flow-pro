Role: Doctor

- `198` I always recommend a crown as the treatment option with the highest long-term success rate for primary tooth lesions that are structurally high-risk - specifically MO lesions on first primary molars, MOD lesions on any molar, lesions more than 50% into dentin, anterior lesions involving 3 or more surfaces or the incisal edge, and lesions of any size in a child being treated under general anesthesia.
- `199` I treatment plan anterior teeth under general anesthesia with heightened caution and transparency, recommending zirconia crowns for restorable teeth or extraction when prognosis is poor.

- `204` I always include an indirect pulp cap (IPC) in the treatment plan when a lesion approximates the pulp and pulp protection may be needed.
- `205` I always adhere to the AAPD Maximum Recommended Dose (MRD) and weight-based dosing guidelines for all oral sedation medications and never exceed these limits.
- `4012` I always offer definitive restorative treatment (composite, crown, EXT) for lesions extending past the DEJ.
- `4013` I always offer both white and silver crown options when full-coverage restorations are indicated.
- `4014` When a primary molar is extracted or planned for extraction, I always evaluate whether space maintenance is indicated based on the patient's age, dental development, eruption status, and clinical circumstances, and document the decision and family discussion when applicable.
- `4016` I always evaluate the need for an ortho referral around 7-8 and again around 12-13 - and an oral surgery referral for 3rds around 15-16
- `4044` When a child is tolerating treatment well and time permits, I always ask the parent's permission mid-appointment to complete additional planned treatment the same day.
