# Email draft: CD update on regional coaching changes

**To:** Dr. Alex, Kasey
**Subject:** ProMoves updates: regional coaches and a smoother coaching flow

Hey Alex and Kasey,

We just shipped a round of updates to the clinical side of ProMoves, built around bringing on our first regional clinical coaches. Quick bullets so you can skim.

**Assigning a coach to a doctor** (the one thing I need you to own)

- On any doctor's page, use the Doctor Coaches card near the top to assign their coach.
- Once assigned, that coach sees a version of the Clinical portal scoped to just their doctors and can run the full loop: baseline, agenda, invite, meeting summary.
- **Assign one coach per doctor.** The system supports more, but treat one named coach as the rule.

**Action steps, front and center**

- The next steps you and the doctor agree on at the end of a conversation are still entered the same way at meeting write-up. Nothing new to learn.
- New: each action step can be linked to a Pro Move. The ones slated for that session are offered first, one tap. If the conversation branched, take a quick look for a matching Pro Move before entering it. If nothing fits, skip it.
- The latest action steps now show on your doctor's page too, not just on the doctor's home screen, with their Pro Move tags.
- Next session, both of you report how they went, same as before.

**Baseline review prep**

- Once a doctor finishes their self-assessment, a Baseline Review Prep button appears on their page in your Clinical portal.
- It unlocks after you finish your own observed baseline, then puts their self-ratings next to yours and ranks the biggest gaps in both directions. Built for prepping the first conversation.
- Tip baked into the app: in the conversation, name the Pro Move, not the number you gave it.

**Keeping a rhythm**

- The doctor roster now shows time since each doctor's last session. It turns amber past about a month.
- When you write up a meeting, it asks when you'll meet next. Give it a date and the next session is created for you.

**A new last step when you finish a meeting**

- After you submit the summary: a quick coach self-assessment, five sliders about how you coached.
- Private to you, always skippable. The point is the half minute of reflection.

**Smaller things you'll notice**

- The baseline wizard opens with a short "before you start" guide the first time.
- The doctor's welcome letter now tells them up front that their coach completes an observed baseline, and why.
- Editing an already-published agenda no longer risks clearing it (real bug, fixed).
- Meeting dates now show when you met, not when you wrote it up.
- Transcripts pasted into the AI assist are saved with the record.
- Sessions are named the same on both sides now: Check-in 1, Check-in 2, and so on.

Poke around and reply with anything that feels off.

John
