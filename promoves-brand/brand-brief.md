# PRO MOVES — Brand Brief (the invariant · needs Johno's edit before design runs)

## What Pro Moves is
The coaching and professional-development platform used every week inside Alcan Dental Cooperative practices. It runs the weekly loop (three skills, self-evaluation, coach facilitation), quarterly evaluations across the four domains (clinical, clerical, cultural, case acceptance), "glows & grows" feedback, and the pay-band transparency system. Its core mechanic — and arguably its soul — is **the delta**: closing the gap between how you see yourself and how you're observed. Its promise: **turning a job into a career.**

## Brand architecture (the sister-brand relationship)
- **Alcan Dental Cooperative** — the parent. Established mark: angular mountain peaks + a gray road, navy `#113B62` / blue `#005286` / 40% gray `#929497`, Biondi Sans.
- **Pro Moves** — the sister brand being created now. Must read as *family* to Alcan (same typographic voice, palette kinship) while standing alone: it appears daily on staff phones and screens, on MyProMoves.com, and on a conference stage where Alcan may not be explained.
- **EduStack** — the commercial sibling; separate brand, not in scope, but Pro Moves should not collide with it.

## Audiences, in priority order
1. **Alcan staff** — front desk, assistants, office managers, doctors — who open it weekly on phones and desktops. It must feel like *theirs*: aspirational but warm, a career companion, never corporate surveillance.
2. **Practice leaders/coaches** who run the loop.
3. **Outsiders** — conference audiences and MyProMoves.com visitors — for whom the mark must look credible next to venture-backed software.

## What the brand must carry (meaning, not prescriptions)
Growth and progression (neophyte → expert in 16 weeks) · the delta between self-perception and observed reality · gentle pressure, relentlessly applied (steady drip, not rah-rah) · craft and professionalism (a "pro move" is a learnable, nameable skill) · kinship with Alcan's mountain without being a smaller copy of it.

## Hard constraints
- Biondi Sans is the brand typeface (shared with Alcan; weights available: Ultra Light → Bold).
- Palette must be *family* to Alcan's navy/blue/gray — extensions and accents allowed, replacements are not.
- The mark must survive: 16px favicon · 1024px app icon · one-color/mono · on dark (the deck's charcoal) and on white (the app's light UI) · embroidered-on-a-scrub-pocket simplicity.
- Works beside the Alcan logo in a lockup without visual argument.

## Judging criteria (same as the deck, plus one)
Modern · Premium · Considered · Intentional · **Warm** (staff must love wearing it, not just respect it).
