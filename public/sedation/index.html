About the percentile

BMI and the CDC BMI-for-age percentile calculate automatically from sex, date of birth, measurement date, weight, and height. The percentile applies to children ages 2 through 19 and uses the CDC sex- and age-specific LMS reference values. For results at or above the 95th percentile, the CDC extended BMI-for-age method is used.
